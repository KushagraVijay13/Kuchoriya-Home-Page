## 4. Featured Portfolio & Case Studies

All four projects presented in the redesign are real-world platforms engineered by Kuchoriya TechSoft and listed on their official portfolio and case-study directories:

### 1. HIPAA-Compliant EMR Integration Platform
- **Industry:** Healthcare & MedTech
- **Verified URL:** `https://kuchoriyatechsoft.com/case-studies/hipaa-compliant-emr-integration`
- **Scope:** Centralized medical record gateway unifying multiple disparate EHR systems using HL7/FHIR protocols, AES-256 encryption, and clinical analytics.
- **Results Substantiation:** 100% HIPAA/HITECH compliance, 40% reduction in physician charting overhead, sub-second query response over 2M+ records.

### 2. eduK Online Learning & Creator Platform
- **Industry:** Education & EdTech
- **Verified URL:** `https://kuchoriyatechsoft.com/portfolio/eduk`
- **Scope:** High-concurrency video delivery platform and AI-driven skill-recommendation engine built for 500,000+ creators across Latin America (Brazil).
- **Results Substantiation:** 500k+ active users, 35% higher course completion rate, 60% CDN bandwidth savings via adaptive HLS streaming.

### 3. Expensya Autonomous Expense Platform
- **Industry:** FinTech & Enterprise SaaS
- **Verified URL:** `https://kuchoriyatechsoft.com/portfolio/expensya`
- **Scope:** AI-driven expense management platform with computer vision receipt OCR, multi-currency corporate card sync, and SAP/Oracle ERP connectors.
- **Results Substantiation:** 80% reduction in expense reimbursement processing cycle, 99.2% line-item OCR recognition accuracy.

### 4. Sixt Rent a Car Mobility App
- **Industry:** Travel, Mobility & Automotive
- **Verified URL:** `https://kuchoriyatechsoft.com/portfolio/sixt`
- **Scope:** Mobile application for real-time fleet availability, dynamic lot pricing, digital contract signing, and contactless vehicle unlock via Bluetooth/NFC.
- **Results Substantiation:** 4.8 / 5.0 average app store rating, 65%+ adoption of contactless pickup at airport hubs.
